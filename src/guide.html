Cervical Spine Ankylosis Study

How to read the cases — a short picture guide.

There is nothing to install. The images stay on your own computer and nothing is sent over the internet. If you get stuck at any point, stop and contact us rather than guessing — nothing you do can damage the images.
1

Put the files together in one folder

You will receive one file called Ankylosis-Study.html and several files called CASES-batch-1.zip, CASES-batch-2.zip and so on.

  1. Make a new folder on your Desktop called ANKYLOSIS STUDY.
  2. Move Ankylosis-Study.html into it.
  3. Right-click each zip file, choose Extract All…, and extract it into that same folder.

All the batches join together into one folder called CASES. When you are done it should look like this:

ANKYLOSIS STUDY
Ankylosis-Study.html ← you open this one
CASES ← you point the program at this one
Case-001
Case-002
Case-003 … and so on

You can delete the zip files afterwards if you need the space.

2

Open the program

Double-click Ankylosis-Study.html. It opens in your web browser. Please use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. You will see this:

Cervical Spine Ankylosis Study

Click the button below to begin.
Open the image folder
Choose the folder named CASES. Your browser will ask whether to upload the folder — this is a standard message, nothing leaves your computer.

Click Open the image folder, then choose the folder called CASES and click Select Folder.

You will then see a message asking whether to upload the folder. This wording comes from the browser itself, not from us. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — it is only asking permission to read files already on your computer. Click Upload to continue.

The first case appears after a few seconds.

Moving around the images:

Move through the imagesDrag the Image slider, or click ‹ › (hold to keep going). The mouse wheel and ↑ ↓ keys also work.
Adjust brightness / contrastDrag the Brightness and Contrast sliders. Or hold the left mouse button on the picture and move the mouse.
Zoom in and outDrag the Zoom slider, or hold Ctrl and roll the mouse wheel
Slide the picture aroundHold the right mouse button on the picture and move the mouse
Put everything back to normalClick Reset view, or Bone / Soft tissue for the standard settings
4

Recording your findings

For every level from C1 to C7, choose one of three answers:

YesAnkylosis is present at this level.
NoAnkylosis is not present at this level.
Can't tellThis level cannot be judged — for example it is not fully included in the scan, or artefact obscures it. Please use this rather than guessing.

Then choose how confident you are about the case overall (Low, Moderate or High).

The Next case button only turns blue once every level has been answered. Anything still missing is listed underneath it. To change an answer, simply click a different button. To revisit an earlier case, click Back.

5

Saving and sending your work

Your answers are saved automatically as you go. You may close the page at any time and come back later — it will continue from where you stopped.

At the end of every session, click “Save results file” at the top right. A file is placed in your Downloads folder with a name like ankylosis-FNGHKG-20260918-1430.csv. Please email that file back to us.

It does not matter if you send it more than once, or if you have not finished all the cases yet. Just always send the most recent file.

?

If something goes wrong

It says no images were found
The wrong folder was chosen. Click the button again and select the folder named CASES — not one of the Case-001 folders inside it, and not a zip file.
The page is blank, or it opened in the wrong program
Right-click Ankylosis-Study.html, choose Open with, and select Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
It takes a while to open a case
Normal for the first case. After that, the next case is quietly prepared in the background while you are reading, so it should appear straight away.
I think I lost my answers
Look in your Downloads folder for a file whose name starts with ankylosis-backup. Open the program and click Restore from a backup file at the bottom of the first screen.
Anything else
Please contact us before carrying on, and mention the six-letter code shown at the top right of the screen.
Thank you for taking part. Please read the cases in the order the program presents them, and do not discuss individual cases with the other readers while the study is running.
